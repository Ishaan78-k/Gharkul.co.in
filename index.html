Founder – Mr. Sanjeev Ganpat Bhabal

Mr. Sanjeev Ganpat Bhabal is the founder and proprietor of Gharkul Real Estate Consultancy. A Mumbai University graduate (1993), he has been running a real estate consultancy and advisory firm since 1994.

He is recognised not as a salesperson but as a valuable advisor and real estate consultant. His understanding of the area and its properties lets him offer the best options for each client, with an honest approach to the sale and purchase of residential and commercial properties and a win-win outcome as the aim.

Our logo shows a bird picking a dreamstick to build a home. We stand for the same: helping buyers build their homes and turning our promise into reality.
